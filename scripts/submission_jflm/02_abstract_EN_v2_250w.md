**Title:** Development and Retrospective Single-Expert Validation of the TOMEC (Trauma–Obstetric Medico-legal Causality) Score for Standardised Causality Assessment in Pregnancy-Related Trauma Leading to Preterm Birth or Pregnancy Loss: A Pilot Study on 3,501 Turkish Court Decisions

**Background and Aim.** Forensic causality assessment in pregnancy-related trauma under Turkish Penal Code Articles 87 and 88 currently lacks a standardised, reproducible scoring framework. Existing obstetric trauma scores predict clinical outcome but do not address medico-legal causality attribution. We aimed to develop and pilot-test the TOMEC (Trauma–Obstetric Medico-legal Causality) Score.

**Materials and Methods.** TOMEC is a five-domain weighted score [0–100]: Trauma severity (T, 25%), Obstetric/gestational status (O, 20%), Maternal comorbidities (M, 15%), Energy/event mechanism (E, 20%), Chronological/temporal proximity (C, 20%), with seven causality categories (Definite to None). The score was applied retrospectively to a corpus of 3,501 Turkish court decisions (Yargıtay, Danıştay, AYM, AİHM) retrieved through systematic public-record search. After multi-stage filtering (regex strict + obstetric-motif requirement), 313 obstetric-relevant decisions were retained. Inter-rater reliability was assessed on a stratified n=35 sub-sample by the developer (single forensic medicine expert) and an independent large language model (OpenAI GPT-4o, temperature = 0) under double-blind conditions.

**Results.** Observed agreement was 82.9% (29/35). Cohen κ = 0.732 (95% bootstrap CI 0.523–0.909, B=2000). Linear-weighted κ = 0.736; quadratic-weighted κ = 0.738; Krippendorff α = 0.733. Macro-F1 = 0.818; weighted-F1 = 0.824. The PARTIAL category showed the lowest agreement (per-class F1 = 0.769).

**Conclusion.** TOMEC demonstrates substantial single-expert with LLM-assisted preliminary reliability for medico-legal causality classification in pregnancy-related trauma cases. Prospective multi-expert validation is required before clinical-forensic deployment.

**Keywords:** forensic obstetrics; medico-legal causality; trauma in pregnancy; inter-rater reliability; clinical scoring system; Turkish Penal Code
